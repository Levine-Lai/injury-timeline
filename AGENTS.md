# Injury Timeline UI Rules

- Every visible label must help the user identify a player, compare evidence, or make a decision. Remove filler, repeated headings, and decorative explanations.
- Do not place secondary descriptions in the top-right corner of cards. Reserve that position for an essential status, date, value, or action only.
- Keep the information order fixed: player identity and current injury, recovery estimate, then comparable cases.
- Use light surfaces with blue as the primary color, green for positive signals, and red for risk. Do not use black backgrounds or large dark color blocks.
- One card should serve one task. Avoid decorative badges, gradients, icons, shadows, and labels that do not encode information.
- Put data limitations and source notes once in a compact footer or source area instead of repeating them across cards.
- Never present an inferred recovery period as a medical fact. If the injury type or date is missing, show a clean unavailable state instead of inventing a value.
